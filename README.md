## 🖱️ How to Use the Interface

The interface stays simple. You select your preferred AI model from the drop-down menu at the top of the window. Type your prompt into the text box at the bottom. Press the Enter key or click the send button to transmit your request.

The system processes your text and returns a response in the main window. You can copy this text to your clipboard or save it to a file. The application keeps a history of your past requests in the sidebar. You can review or clear this history at any time.

## 🔑 Setting Up Your API Credentials

aisuite acts as a messenger between you and AI companies. Most of these companies require a specific key to verify your identity.

1.  Open the Settings menu inside aisuite.
2.  Choose the API Keys tab.
3.  Locate the entry for your desired provider.
4.  Paste your personal key into the box.
5.  Save your changes to apply the settings.

You obtain these keys by registering for a free or paid account on the websites of the AI providers. The application supports major providers. You can store multiple keys and switch between models without re-entering your information.
